About

Zhejiang University · Landscape Architecture · Class of 2025

I studied landscape architecture for four years, which trained me to read environments—how people move through space, what makes a place feel right or wrong, why some designs hold attention and others don't. That kind of observational habit doesn't turn off when I close a design software.

Somewhere in my final year, I started noticing how much friction existed between design workflows and AI tools. Prompts getting lost. Research scattered. No tool built with designers in mind. So I built one. That was PromptDeck—my first end-to-end product, shipped in a week.

I'm now actively transitioning into AI product and applied AI roles. I don't have a traditional tech background, but I have a trained eye, a research mindset, and enough stubbornness to ship things. Currently based in Hangzhou, open to remote.
